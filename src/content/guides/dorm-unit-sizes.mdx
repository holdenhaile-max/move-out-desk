A dorm is one room, not an apartment. CubeSmart's college page says that packing up an entire dorm starts with at least a 5x5. Their size guide says a 10x10 is the size for a one-bedroom apartment. Checked Nov 21, 2026. SmartStop's size guide calls a 5x5 a hall closet and a 5x10 a walk-in closet. Checked Nov 23, 2026. The Texas Ave prices are on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue). Price the small rooms first. Step up only if you are keeping a couch, a mattress and a desk together, or a whole apartment.

The words below are what the companies publish. The prices are the College Station and Bryan lists.

## What they say each size holds

**5x5.** CubeSmart's size chart calls a 5x5 a hall closet, 200 cubic feet. The paragraph on the same page calls it a small walk-in closet, 5 feet by 5 feet by 8 feet, and says it holds boxes, end tables, lamps, clothing, and up to 35 file boxes. Their college page says an entire dorm starts here. Checked Nov 21, 2026. U-Haul at Texas Ave labels its 5x5 "Miscellaneous Storage." Checked Nov 23, 2026. Premium Spaces, the company that runs Lock N Roll, calls a 5x5 a hall closet for boxes, seasonal decor, and a few small items. That page also says 5x5 rooms start at $39 somewhere in the company. That starting price is not the College Station rate. Checked Nov 21, 2026.

**5x10.** CubeSmart says this is 5 feet by 10 feet by 8 feet, 400 cubic feet, about a large walk-in closet. They say it holds the contents of one room: a TV, shelves, a bedroom set, and a few boxes, and that a queen or twin mattress can stand up in it. Checked Nov 21, 2026. U-Haul labels its 5x10 "1-2 Bedroom Home." That label is bigger than a dorm. Checked Nov 23, 2026. Premium Spaces calls a 5x10 a walk-in closet that holds a small one-bedroom apartment minus large appliances, including a queen mattress, a small dresser, a desk, and 15 to 20 medium boxes. Their company starting price is $65. Lock N Roll's own list is lower. Checked Nov 21, 2026.

**10x10.** CubeSmart says this is about an average bedroom or a one-car garage, 800 cubic feet, and that it holds a one-bedroom apartment: a king mattress set, dressers, couches, or a refrigerator and a washer. Checked Nov 21, 2026. U-Haul labels its 10x10 "2-3 Bedroom Home." Checked Nov 23, 2026. Premium Spaces calls a 10x10 half of a single-car garage and says it fits a one-bedroom apartment with appliances. Their company starting price is $119. Checked Nov 21, 2026. A dorm does not need this room unless you are storing a couch or a full apartment's furniture.

**Bigger than a dorm.** CubeSmart says a 10x15 holds a two-bedroom apartment and a 10x20 is about a one-car garage. U-Haul labels a 10x15 "2-3 Bedroom Home" and a 10x20 "4 Bedroom Home or larger." Skip these for boxes, a mini fridge, and a bike.

Read the monthly rate, then ask about a move-in fee if the card does not print one. Checked Nov 22, 2026, Lock N Roll's unit cards show the monthly rate and do not print a separate move-in fee. The company page still says there is a small lock fee and does not print the amount. On that same list, a climate 5x5 is $37, an interior 5x5 that is not climate controlled is $29, a climate 5x10 is $64, and a climate 10x10 is $128.

U-Haul's monthly price is locked for 12 months and the admin fee is $0. Checked Nov 23, 2026. SpareBox and CubeSmart show lower numbers that the pages do not lock for a summer. Those warnings are on the [cheapest storage page](/guides/cheapest-storage-near-texas-am). If the things cannot take July heat, use the [climate comparison](/guides/college-station-climate-controlled).

## If you are not renting a room

Dorm Room Movers' box is 18 by 18 by 22 inches, 4.125 cubic feet. They say one box holds a bedding set, including pillows. The Texas A&M price for that box is $91 for 4 months, or $81.90 if you prepay. Checked Nov 21, 2026. Five of those boxes are a pickup order, not a 5x5. The pickup versus a room is the [summer storage guide](/guides/texas-am-summer-storage).
