Call Collegeboxes before you pay. Their Texas A&M page says room pickup is currently unavailable, and it also lists pickup dates. Those are two different answers. This page is the dates, the $56 box, the $37 fee, and what to ask.

The school page says it was modified on November 5, 2026. Checked Nov 20, 2026. It is not an official Texas A&M service. It prints "No Active Partnership with Texas A&M University, College Station."

## The page says two different things about pickup

The opening text says Collegeboxes offers direct shipping at Texas A&M, and that full-service moving (pickup from the room) is currently unavailable. A later block says students drop packed items at an on-campus location, U-Haul stores them, and they are delivered to rooms in the fall. The pickup calendar and the FAQ say something else again: belongings are picked up from the dorm room, and you or a proxy have to be there.

The calendar that is actually printed:

- Pickup on Friday, December 11, 2026, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
- Pickup on Monday, May 10, 2027, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
- Delivery to the room on Tuesday, January 19, 2027, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
- For Ship to School, sign up by Thursday, January 7, 2027, for that January 19 delivery.

A different date, or an off-campus pickup, is "contact us." The national fee list prices a special pickup or delivery at $500. The school page does not repeat that $500 next to the contact-us line. A self-service drop-off at the storage location is $25 on the national list.

The school page also has a sentence about storage near Northeastern University in Boston. Ignore it. Confirm your College Station order in writing.

Do not count on room pickup until they tell you that service is open.

## Where the boxes would sit

The FAQ names the building: U-Haul Moving & Storage at Texas Ave, 2813 Texas Ave, College Station, TX 77845, at Texas Ave and Harvey Mitchell Parkway S, near Bee Creek Park. Collegeboxes says that is just over 3 miles from campus, southeast on Texas Ave.

Collegeboxes says you can get to stored items during business hours if you give three business days' notice. It also says the U-Haul is open Monday through Saturday 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Sunday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. U-Haul's own page for the same building says Friday runs until 8 p.m., access for renters is 5 a.m. to 10 p.m., and climate-controlled rooms (heated and cooled) are held between 65 and 85 degrees. Use U-Haul for the building's hours and temperature. Use Collegeboxes for the claim that your Collegeboxes items are in that building.

You can also skip Collegeboxes and rent a room in that building yourself. A climate-controlled 5x10 is $99.95 a month, the admin fee (a one-time move-in charge) is $0, and the rate is locked for 12 months. Checked Nov 21, 2026. That is a different purchase. Collegeboxes is selling labor and a return delivery. U-Haul is selling the room.

## The $56 and the $37 are not one number

The figure people repeat, about $56 a box, is real. It is on the national pricing page, not on the Texas A&M page. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

For a 4-month term, with pickup and delivery included and no registration fee, the national list says:

- Small loose items, such as a lamp, fan, or microwave: $50. This is not the $56 row.
- A Collegebox, a box smaller than 24 by 16 by 18 inches, the supply-kit outer box, or a U-Haul small, medium, or large moving box: $56.
- A suitcase, bin, nightstand, or similar: $76.
- A mini fridge, or a box larger than 24 by 16 by 18 inches up to 8 cubic feet: $82.
- A bicycle, desk, chair, or twin mattress: $88.
- A futon, couch, or unboxed television: $130.

The Texas A&M page's dollar figure is different. It says a $37 handling fee (a charge for receiving a box) applies for each box Collegeboxes receives for you. The national pricing page, in the Ship to School section, says the handling fee is $30 a box and that it includes receiving the shipment, up to one month of storage near campus, and delivery to the room. Both numbers are published. They do not match.

Nothing on either page says "add $37 to the $56." They may be different products. Five boxes at $56 are $280 if you are buying the national storage rate. Five boxes at $56 plus five handling fees of $37 are $465 if they bill you both. Ask which bill you are on. The calculator does not add them unless you choose that option.

## The supply kit

The Signature Supply Kit is $39.99 plus tax, shipped to you. Checked Nov 20, 2026. It includes five reusable boxes, 24 by 16 by 18 inches, one roll of U-Haul tape, five zip ties, one flat bonus box that is 36 by 24 by 7 inches, a marker, and ten FedEx label pouches. The page says kits are packed and shipped as soon as you buy them, so this is not a kit that waits for move-out week.

You do not have to buy it to understand the $56 rate. The $56 tier explicitly includes their box and ordinary U-Haul moving boxes. The kit is packaging, not storage.

## What five boxes cost

**Storage rate only, national list.** 5 times $56 is $280 for four months, pickup and delivery included, if that list is what Texas A&M students are sold. The school page does not reprint $56, so whether it applies is the soft part. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**Ship to School handling only.** 5 times $37 is $185 on the school page, or 5 times $30 is $150 on the national page. That fee, as described nationally, is receipt plus up to one month of storage plus room delivery. It is not a summer-long storage price. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**Supply kit on top.** Add $39.99 plus tax if you buy the kit. Tax is unverified. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**Compared with Dorm Room Movers for the same five boxes.** Their Texas A&M list prices a DRM box at $91, or $81.90 prepaid, plus $49.99 to register. Five prepaid boxes plus registration are $459.49. Their example order of five boxes is $409.50 before registration. Checked Nov 21, 2026. The head-to-head is [Collegeboxes vs Dorm Room Movers](/compare/collegeboxes-vs-dorm-room-movers).

**Compared with renting the same building.** Four months of a climate 5x10 at U-Haul Texas Ave is $399.80 with a published price lock. Checked Nov 21, 2026. You do the carrying. You can visit during access hours without booking three days ahead.

## Shipping home, if you are not coming back

The national Ship Home estimates are $45 to $60 per box in-state, $60 to $90 regional, and $75 to $100 coast to coast. Checked Nov 20, 2026. The page does not list which states count as regional. That section says FedEx pickup and delivery are included and that there is no handling fee. Your exact price depends on destination, dimensions, and weight. A quote to a named address is unverified. Direct Shipping on the school page is described as FedEx pickup with no storage and no in-room pickup.

## What to confirm before you pay Collegeboxes

1. Is full-service dorm pickup open for my date, or only shipping and drop-off?
2. Which of December 11, 2026, May 10, 2027, and January 19, 2027 applies to me?
3. Is my box on the $56 storage rate, the $37 receiving fee, or both?
4. Are the items stored at 2813 Texas Ave, and can I get to them with three days' notice?
5. What is the coverage amount on my order, $100 or something else?
6. If I live off campus, what does pickup cost? The page does not say.

If the answers are vague, the local unit table does not depend on a pickup calendar. It is the [College Station climate comparison](/guides/college-station-climate-controlled).
