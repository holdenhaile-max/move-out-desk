Northgate is the blocks along University Drive on the north side of campus. None of the storage buildings below sit on that street. If you live at White Creek, use the [White Creek page](/guides/storage-near-white-creek). The miles from there are different. If you live there, the useful question is how far you have to drive, and what a small room costs when you get there.

Miles below are a driving route from U Center Apartments at Northgate, the OpenStreetMap place with that name, to the building pin. Checked Nov 22, 2026. Your own drive can differ by a turn or two. Dowling Road is not in the mile list. A building pin for 10257 N. Dowling Road was not confirmed, so this page does not invent one.

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan. 0.85 miles.** This is the closest room with a published price. A 5x5 is $42 and the page marked it sold out. A 5x10 is $46 and the page marked it in stock. Those lines do not say whether the room is heated and cooled. The facility page does say it has temperature-controlled indoor units and month-to-month leases for students between semesters. Ask which price is the heated room before you book it for clothes or books. Prices checked Nov 25, 2026.

**U-Haul at 501 Texas Ave S. 1.06 miles.** This counter is close, and it does not solve the storage problem. The page lists moving trucks, propane, and trucks for sale. It does not list a storage room or a monthly storage price. Checked Nov 22, 2026. The U-Haul rooms are at 2813 Texas Ave S, 4.02 miles on the same kind of route. A climate 5x10 there is $99.95 a month, locked for 12 months, with a $0 admin fee. That room price was checked Nov 25, 2026. The mile figure was checked Nov 22, 2026. SmartStop is farther down the same road, at 3101 Texas Ave S. This page does not add a mile for it. The prices are on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue).

**CubeSmart, 104 Holleman Drive. 2.56 miles.** Checked Nov 24, 2026, on Storage.com, the lowest drive-up 5x5 is $15.60 online and $26 in the store, and the lowest drive-up 5x10 is $31.80 online and $53 in the store. No heated room was on that list. Checked Nov 25, 2026, CubeSmart's own page did not list a 5x5 or a 5x10. It did list a drive-up 10x10 at $54.60 online and $91 in the store, and it says the store is less than three miles from campus. That is a campus claim, not a Northgate claim. The mile figure was checked Nov 22, 2026.

**CubeSmart, 219 Manuel Drive. 2.75 miles.** Climate 5x5 is $24.60 online and $41 in the store. Climate 5x10 is $57.60 online and $96 in the store. The banner says first month free and 40% off. The page does not say the online price lasts the summer. Use $96 for a climate 5x10 unless they lock the online price in writing. Storage.com lists a $29 admin fee. CubeSmart's own page did not open. Prices checked Nov 24, 2026. The mile figure was checked Nov 22, 2026.

**Lock N Roll, 1501 Earl Rudder Freeway. 3.48 miles.** Climate 5x5 is $38. Climate 5x10 is $64. The unit list shows that monthly rate and does not print a separate move-in fee. The company page still says there is a small lock fee and does not print the amount. Prices checked Nov 24, 2026. The mile figure was checked Nov 22, 2026.

**SpareBox, 3201 Earl Rudder Freeway S. 5.07 miles.** Climate 5x5 is $17. Climate 5x10 is $32. Both have an asterisk. The footnote says the rate is for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Prices checked Nov 24, 2026. The mile figure was checked Nov 22, 2026.

**Dowling Road Storage, 10257 N. Dowling Road.** No mile on this page. Climate 5x5 and climate 5x10 are both $74, with 50% off the first month and no price lock. Prices checked Nov 24, 2026. This page still has no mile for Dowling Road.

Sort the table by price. It is every published 5x5 and 5x10, not a ranking by miles. Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Drive-up means you can park at the door.
