The Gardens are on-campus apartments at 250 Calvin Moore Avenue, at University Drive and South College Avenue, near the Engineering Complex. Residence Life lists that address and the office phone (979) 845-2261. The office is open Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Mail does not go to Calvin Moore. Buildings F through L use 302 Ball Street. Buildings M through Q use 1100 Hensel Drive. Checked Nov 27, 2026.

If you live at White Creek, use the [White Creek page](/guides/storage-near-white-creek). If you live at Northgate, use the [Northgate page](/guides/storage-near-northgate). The miles from those places are different.

No storage building is on Calvin Moore Avenue. Miles below are a driving route from the OpenStreetMap pin for 250 Calvin Moore Avenue. SmartStop's mile uses the pin on SmartStop's own page. The other pins are OpenStreetMap. Checked Nov 27, 2026. Your own drive can differ by a turn or two. Dowling Road is not in the mile list. A building pin for 10257 N. Dowling Road was not confirmed, so this page does not invent one.

Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Drive-up means you can park at the door.

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan. 1.15 miles.** This is the closest room with a published price. A 5x5 is $42 and the page marked it sold out. A 5x10 is $46 and the page marked it in stock. Those lines do not say whether the room is heated. The facility page does offer temperature-controlled indoor units and month-to-month storage for Texas A&M students. Ask which price is the heated room before you put clothes in it. Prices checked Nov 27, 2026. Phone is (979) 846-3000.

**CubeSmart, 219 Manuel Drive. 2.51 miles.** This is the closest room that labels a heated price. Climate 5x5 is $24.60 online and $41 in the store. Climate 5x10 is $57.60 online and $96 in the store. The banner says first month free and 40% off. The page does not say the online price lasts past that promotion. Use $96 for a climate 5x10 unless they lock the online price in writing. Prices checked Nov 26, 2026. The mile was checked Nov 27, 2026.

**CubeSmart, 104 Holleman Drive. 2.70 miles.** The own page did not list a 5x5 or a 5x10. It did list a drive-up 10x10 at $54.60 online and $91 in the store. No heated room was on that list. Storage.com still lists a drive-up 5x5 at $15.60 online and $26 in the store, and a drive-up 5x10 at $31.80 online and $53 in the store. Checked Nov 27, 2026.

**Lock N Roll, 1501 Earl Rudder Freeway. 2.75 miles.** The mile was checked Nov 27, 2026. The room prices were checked Nov 26, 2026, and they are in the table with that date.

**U-Haul, 2813 Texas Ave S. 3.78 miles.** The small rooms are inside the building. A heated 5x10 is $99.95 a month, locked for 12 months, and the admin fee is $0. An admin fee is a one-time charge at move-in. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. Checked Nov 27, 2026.

**SmartStop, 3101 Texas Ave S. 4.07 miles.** A heated 5x5 is $18 online and $35 in the store, and 1 was left. You also pay a $29 admin fee. The page does not say the online price lasts past the first month. Checked Nov 27, 2026. The rest of that building is on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue).

SpareBox on Earl Rudder is 4.83 miles. Those prices were checked Nov 26, 2026. This page does not repeat them. The table shows each price with the day it was checked.

If the things cannot take a Texas summer, use a heated room even when a closer room is cheaper. Triangle is closer, and its price lines do not say which room is heated. Manuel Drive does. That list is the [climate page](/guides/college-station-climate-controlled).

The Gardens page says residents pick a 9 1/2 month contract or a 12 month contract. The academic-year dates are approximately August 15 to May 31. The 12 month dates are approximately August 15 to July 31. Those are approximate. Hall closing for a residence hall is a different page. Checked Nov 27, 2026.

The same page says the complex is on the Texas A&M bus route. It does not name the route. A storage room still has to be loaded by someone. If nobody has a car, compare a pickup on the [summer storage page](/guides/texas-am-summer-storage).
