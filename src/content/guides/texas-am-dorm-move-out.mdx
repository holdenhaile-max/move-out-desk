Residence Life has not posted the exact day and time the halls close for Fall 2026. Do not book a truck for a day you invented from the finals calendar. Use the dates below, then call your hall if you need the clock time.

## What is posted

**This December.** Final exams run Monday, December 7 through Thursday, December 10, 2026. Commencement is Friday, December 11 and Saturday, December 12. Checked Nov 20, 2026, on the [academic calendar](https://catalog.tamu.edu/undergraduate/academic-calendar/).

The [move-out page](https://reslife.tamu.edu/moveout/) says halls generally close at 6 p.m. on the Saturday after finals and stay closed over winter break. The same page says the specific Fall 2026 date will be announced. It does not name December 12, or any other day, as the closing time. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**If you are coming back in January.** You do not have to empty the room. Take valuables and anything you need over the break. The building is locked and your card will not open it. Checked Nov 20, 2026, on the move-out page and on the [winter break answer](https://reslifehelp.tamu.edu/hc/en-us/articles/360051133471-Do-I-have-to-move-out-of-the-Residence-Hall-at-Winter-Break).

**This May is not posted as a clock time.** The move-out page still prints last spring's deadline: 6:00 p.m. on Saturday, May 9, 2026. That day has passed. It does not print a Spring 2027 time. The academic calendar puts spring finals on May 6, May 7, May 10, and May 11, 2027, and commencement from Wednesday, May 12 through Saturday, May 15. The housing contract says halls typically close on the last commencement day in May, and that the exact move-out date is announced about six months ahead. Do not treat May 15 as your checkout time until Residence Life posts it. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**Gardens apartments stay open.** Checkout follows your contract. Schedule the appointment at least 72 hours before the contract ends. Express checkout means you clean, drop the key, and you cannot appeal damage or cleaning charges. Checked Nov 20, 2026. If the apartment will not hold everything, the driving miles and small-unit prices are on the [Gardens storage page](/guides/storage-near-the-gardens).

**Corps housing is a different checkout.** If carpet is still in the room after the deadline, the page says it is thrown out and you can be charged $25 plus $17.95 an hour for labor, with a one-hour minimum. A key you do not return is a $100 lock change. Skipping the inventory card signature is a $25 charge. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

Summer 1 and Summer 2 checkout days on that page are Tuesday, June 30 and Saturday, August 8, in the section that also mentions Fall 2026. The page does not say 2027. Do not use those days as next summer's deadline.

## Checkout, in plain steps

Go to the closing floor meeting. That is where you get the Winter Break Departure form or the Checkout form, and the Express Checkout form in May. If you miss it, contact your resident advisor or the hall director. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

Before you leave a residence hall room:

- Clean the door, floor, windows, blinds, desk, dresser, and bathroom. Take stickers and tape off the walls.
- Put the bed back together, mattress flat, about 6 inches off the wall. Unstack furniture and pull it off the wall.
- If you rented a fridge, defrost it, dry it, leave the doors open, and leave it in the room.
- If the room has a thermostat, set it to 76 degrees and the fan to low.
- Drop your key in the hall drop box if you are using Express Checkout. You can do that any time. You are accepting the charges they find later.

A parent can use the one-hour loading zones Residence Life sets up next to the halls. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

## If you need the stuff during the break

You cannot go back into the hall for a coat or a laptop. If those things are not going home with you, rent a month-to-month room. What one month costs is the [short stay page](/guides/short-term-storage-near-texas-am). CubeSmart's college page says packing up an entire dorm starts with at least a 5x5. What each size holds is the [unit size guide](/guides/dorm-unit-sizes). Checked Nov 21, 2026. Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Sort the table by price. A promo price is not the summer bill unless the place locks it in writing. SmartStop on Texas Avenue is one of the rooms in that table. The online price and the in-store price are on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue).
