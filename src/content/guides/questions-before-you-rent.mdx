A low monthly rate is not the amount that leaves your account. Around College Station, the missing pieces are the same five things. Ask them in this order, and write down the answer with the person's name.

## 1. What is the rate in month two?

U-Haul at Texas Ave prints a 12-month price lock, so month two is the rate on the page. CubeSmart prints an online price and a higher in-store price under a first-month-free banner, and does not say how many months the discount bills. SpareBox says promotional rates are for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Dowling Road offers 50% off the first month and does not mention a lock.

The question: "If I stay four months, what will the card be charged in each month, in dollars?"

## 2. What is the admin fee, the lock, and the insurance?

CubeSmart says you pay the first month plus a one-time administrative fee, and that insurance is required. The Manuel Drive and Holleman Drive pages do not print either dollar amount. You can bring a declaration page if you already have coverage. U-Haul Texas Ave says the admin fee is $0 and there is no deposit. A lock is free with online move-in, listed value $11.95, or you buy one at the counter. SpareBox says an administrative fee applies and does not name it. Dowling's U-Haul listing and Triangle's page, as we could read them, do not print an admin fee. That is not the same thing as a $0 fee.

The question: "What do I pay on day one besides the first month, including tax, lock, and protection?"

## 3. Is this room on the climate system, and what is the setting?

Only U-Haul Texas Ave publishes a band: 65 to 85 degrees, humidity controlled. CubeSmart, SpareBox, and Dowling label specific rooms as climate controlled and do not give degrees. Triangle advertises temperature control and does not label which listed price is that room. Lock N Roll says it has climate-controlled units and does not show a readable price.

The question: "In July, what temperature is this unit held to, and is humidity controlled?"

## 4. Can I get in on the day I move?

Access hours we could verify: U-Haul Texas Ave 5 a.m. to 10 p.m., CubeSmart 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., Dowling 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., SpareBox 6 a.m. to 9 p.m., Triangle 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. Lock N Roll's hours were not in the page content. Office hours are shorter than access hours at every place that publishes both. If you need a lock sold to you, you need the office, not just the gate.

## 5. Will you take a shipped box?

Holleman CubeSmart, Dowling, and Lock N Roll say deliveries are accepted. The others in this set do not say so on the pages we used. If a parent is shipping a box to the unit, ask for the address format and whether someone will sign.
