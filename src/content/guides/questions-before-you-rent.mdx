The monthly price is not what leaves your account. Ask these five things, in order, and write down the answer with the person's name.

## 1. What do you charge in month two?

U-Haul at Texas Ave prints a 12-month price lock, so month two is the price on the page. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. CubeSmart shows an online price and a higher in-store price under a first-month-free banner, and does not say how many months the discount lasts. SpareBox says promo prices are for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Dowling Road offers 50% off the first month and does not mention a lock. CubeSmart on Manuel Drive, SpareBox, and Dowling were read on their own pages. Checked Nov 26, 2026. Holleman's own page opened on Nov 25, 2026, and it still does not say how many months the online price lasts. U-Haul checked Nov 25, 2026.

Ask: "If I stay four months, what will the card be charged each month, in dollars?"

## 2. What is the move-in fee, the lock, and the insurance?

An admin fee is a one-time charge when you move in. Storage.com lists a $29 admin fee at CubeSmart on Manuel Drive and at CubeSmart on Holleman Drive, and it says insurance is required. It does not print the insurance price. Manuel Drive's own page, checked Nov 26, 2026, says insurance is required and names an administrative fee, and it does not print either dollar amount. The $29 figure is the Storage.com listing, also checked Nov 26, 2026. Holleman's own page, checked Nov 25, 2026, says insurance is required and names an administrative fee, and it does not print either dollar amount. SpareBox says an admin fee applies and does not name it. Dowling's listing still does not print an admin fee. Both were checked Nov 26, 2026. Triangle still did not print an admin fee. Checked Nov 25, 2026. That is not the same as a $0 fee. U-Haul Texas Ave says the admin fee is $0 and there is no deposit. A lock is free with online move-in, listed value $11.95, or you buy one at the counter. Checked Nov 25, 2026. SmartStop on Texas Avenue prints a $29 admin fee on the move-in panel. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Lock N Roll's unit list charges a $24 administrative fee at move-in. The company pricing page says there is no admin fee beyond the first month and a small lock fee, and it does not print the lock amount. Checked Nov 26, 2026. The missing dollar amounts at SpareBox, Dowling, and Triangle stay unverified. CubeSmart's insurance price stays unverified too.

Ask: "What do I pay on day one besides the first month, including tax, lock, and protection?"

## 3. Is this room heated and cooled, and to what temperature?

Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. U-Haul Texas Ave publishes 65 to 85 degrees and says the room is humidity controlled. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Dowling publishes the same 65 to 85 degrees and says it depends on the system. Checked Nov 26, 2026. CubeSmart and SpareBox label specific rooms as climate controlled and do not give degrees. Triangle advertises temperature control and does not say which listed price is that room. Lock N Roll labels climate rooms on the unit list and does not publish a temperature. Those labels were checked Nov 26, 2026.

Ask: "In July, what temperature is this unit held to, and is humidity controlled?"

## 4. Can I get in on move day?

Hours you can count on: CubeSmart on Manuel Drive is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., and Dowling is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 26, 2026. Holleman's own page also says access is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 25, 2026. U-Haul Texas Ave access is 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 25, 2026. SmartStop on Texas Avenue opens the gate from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 25, 2026. SpareBox is 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. seven days a week. Checked Nov 26, 2026. Triangle's access hours are 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day. Checked Nov 24, 2026. Lock N Roll still does not list a clock time, so those hours stay unverified. Checked Nov 26, 2026. Office hours are shorter than gate hours wherever both are listed. If you need them to sell you a lock, you need the office, not just the gate.

## 5. Will you accept a box my parent ships?

Holleman's page says deliveries are accepted. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Dowling says yes. Lock N Roll's page says deliveries are accepted. Both were checked Nov 26, 2026. The others do not say. If a parent is shipping to the unit, ask how to address it and whether someone will sign.
