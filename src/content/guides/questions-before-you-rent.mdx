The monthly price is not what leaves your account. Ask these five things, in order, and write down the answer with the person's name.

## 1. What do you charge in month two?

U-Haul at Texas Ave prints a 12-month price lock, so month two is the price on the page. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. CubeSmart shows an online price and a higher in-store price under a first-month-free banner, and does not say how many months the discount lasts. SpareBox says promo prices are for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Dowling Road offers 50% off the first month and does not mention a lock. CubeSmart, SpareBox, and Dowling checked Nov 22, 2026. U-Haul checked Nov 23, 2026.

Ask: "If I stay four months, what will the card be charged each month, in dollars?"

## 2. What is the move-in fee, the lock, and the insurance?

An admin fee is a one-time charge when you move in. CubeSmart says you pay the first month plus that fee, and that insurance is required. Manuel Drive and Holleman Drive do not print either dollar amount. Checked Nov 22, 2026. You can bring proof if you already have coverage. SpareBox says an admin fee applies and does not name it. Dowling's listing still does not print an admin fee. Triangle still did not print one. Checked Nov 22, 2026. That is not the same as a $0 fee. U-Haul Texas Ave says the admin fee is $0 and there is no deposit. A lock is free with online move-in, listed value $11.95, or you buy one at the counter. Checked Nov 23, 2026. SmartStop on Texas Avenue prints a $29 admin fee on the move-in panel. Checked Nov 23, 2026. Lock N Roll's unit cards do not print a separate move-in fee. The company page still says there is a small lock fee and does not print the amount. Checked Nov 22, 2026. The missing dollar amounts at CubeSmart, SpareBox, Dowling, Triangle, and Lock N Roll stay unverified.

Ask: "What do I pay on day one besides the first month, including tax, lock, and protection?"

## 3. Is this room heated and cooled, and to what temperature?

Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Only U-Haul Texas Ave publishes a range: 65 to 85 degrees, humidity controlled. CubeSmart, SpareBox, and Dowling label specific rooms as climate controlled and do not give degrees. Triangle advertises temperature control and does not say which listed price is that room. Lock N Roll labels climate rooms on the unit list and does not publish a temperature. CubeSmart, SpareBox, Dowling, and Triangle checked Nov 22, 2026. Lock N Roll's climate labels were checked Nov 22, 2026. U-Haul's temperature band was checked Nov 23, 2026.

Ask: "In July, what temperature is this unit held to, and is humidity controlled?"

## 4. Can I get in on move day?

Hours you can count on: CubeSmart 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. and Dowling 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 22, 2026. U-Haul Texas Ave access is 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 23, 2026. SmartStop on Texas Avenue opens the gate from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 23, 2026. SpareBox is 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. seven days a week. Checked Nov 22, 2026. Triangle's weekly access hours, 6 a.m. to 8 p.m., were checked Nov 19, 2026. Lock N Roll still does not list a clock time, so those hours stay unverified. Checked Nov 22, 2026. Office hours are shorter than gate hours wherever both are listed. If you need them to sell you a lock, you need the office, not just the gate.

## 5. Will you accept a box my parent ships?

Holleman CubeSmart says yes. Checked Nov 22, 2026. Dowling says yes. Checked Nov 22, 2026. Lock N Roll's page says deliveries are accepted. Checked Nov 22, 2026. The others do not say. If a parent is shipping to the unit, ask how to address it and whether someone will sign.
