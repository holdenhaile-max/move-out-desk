The monthly price is not what leaves your account. Ask these five things, in order, and write down the answer with the person's name.

## 1. What do you charge in month two?

U-Haul at Texas Ave prints a 12-month price lock, so month two is the price on the page. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. CubeSmart shows an online price and a higher in-store price under a first-month-free banner, and does not say how many months the discount lasts. SpareBox says promo prices are for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Dowling Road offers 50% off the first month and does not mention a lock. CubeSmart on Manuel Drive was read on Storage.com, because that page did not open. Checked Nov 24, 2026. Holleman's own page opened on Nov 25, 2026, and it still does not say how many months the online price lasts. SpareBox and Dowling were read on their own listings. Checked Nov 24, 2026. U-Haul checked Nov 25, 2026.

Ask: "If I stay four months, what will the card be charged each month, in dollars?"

## 2. What is the move-in fee, the lock, and the insurance?

An admin fee is a one-time charge when you move in. Storage.com lists a $29 admin fee at CubeSmart on Manuel Drive and at CubeSmart on Holleman Drive, and it says insurance is required. It does not print the insurance price. Manuel Drive's own page did not open on Nov 24, 2026. On Nov 22, 2026, that page said you can bring proof if you already have coverage. Holleman's own page, checked Nov 25, 2026, says insurance is required and names an administrative fee, and it does not print either dollar amount. The $29 figure is the Storage.com listing from Nov 24, 2026. SpareBox says an admin fee applies and does not name it. Dowling's listing still does not print an admin fee. Both were checked Nov 24, 2026. Triangle still did not print an admin fee. Checked Nov 25, 2026. That is not the same as a $0 fee. U-Haul Texas Ave says the admin fee is $0 and there is no deposit. A lock is free with online move-in, listed value $11.95, or you buy one at the counter. Checked Nov 25, 2026. SmartStop on Texas Avenue prints a $29 admin fee on the move-in panel. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Lock N Roll's unit list does not print a separate move-in fee. The company page still says there is a small lock fee and does not print the amount. Checked Nov 24, 2026. The missing dollar amounts at SpareBox, Dowling, Triangle, and Lock N Roll stay unverified. CubeSmart's insurance price stays unverified too.

Ask: "What do I pay on day one besides the first month, including tax, lock, and protection?"

## 3. Is this room heated and cooled, and to what temperature?

Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Only U-Haul Texas Ave publishes a range: 65 to 85 degrees, humidity controlled. CubeSmart, SpareBox, and Dowling label specific rooms as climate controlled and do not give degrees. Triangle advertises temperature control and does not say which listed price is that room. Lock N Roll labels climate rooms on the unit list and does not publish a temperature. Those labels were checked Nov 24, 2026. CubeSmart's labels were on Storage.com. U-Haul's temperature band was checked Nov 25, 2026.

Ask: "In July, what temperature is this unit held to, and is humidity controlled?"

## 4. Can I get in on move day?

Hours you can count on: CubeSmart on Manuel Drive is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Storage.com, and Dowling is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 24, 2026. Holleman's own page also says access is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 25, 2026. U-Haul Texas Ave access is 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 25, 2026. SmartStop on Texas Avenue opens the gate from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Checked Nov 25, 2026. SpareBox is 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. seven days a week. Triangle's access hours are 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day. Both were checked Nov 24, 2026. Lock N Roll still does not list a clock time, so those hours stay unverified. Checked Nov 24, 2026. Office hours are shorter than gate hours wherever both are listed. If you need them to sell you a lock, you need the office, not just the gate.

## 5. Will you accept a box my parent ships?

Holleman's page says deliveries are accepted. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Dowling says yes. Lock N Roll's page says deliveries are accepted. Both were checked Nov 24, 2026. The others do not say. If a parent is shipping to the unit, ask how to address it and whether someone will sign.
