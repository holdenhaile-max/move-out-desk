A pickup charges each box to the person who owns it. A unit charges the room to whoever signed. Write the split down before anyone loads the car.

## Use a price you can hold them to

For a shared 5x10 that is labeled climate controlled (heated and cooled), these monthly prices were listed.

- U-Haul Texas Ave, $99.95, locked for 12 months, $0 admin fee. An admin fee is a one-time charge at move-in. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. Checked Nov 27, 2026.
- CubeSmart Manuel Drive, $96 in the store, plus a $29 admin fee on Storage.com. The $57.60 online price is yours only if they confirm the later months. Checked Nov 28, 2026, on Storage.com. CubeSmart's own page did not open. Storage.com listed both prices.
- Dowling Road, $74, with 50% off the first month and no published lock. Checked Nov 27, 2026.
- SpareBox Earl Rudder, $31 promotional. Do not split a summer on that number until they confirm month two. Checked Nov 28, 2026.

Four months at U-Haul is $399.80. Two people is $199.90 each. Three people is $133.27 each. That is before tax, which these pages do not show. Checked Nov 27, 2026. The same four months at CubeSmart's in-store price is $384, plus the $29 admin fee on the Storage.com listing. Checked Nov 28, 2026. Put that fee in the shared pot. Do not leave it on the person who signed.

The [calculator](/guides/texas-am-summer-storage) does this split. Set roommates to 2 or 3 and pick the unit price. Leave "one month free" unchecked unless the place confirmed it.

## Rules that prevent a fight

1. One person signs. These places rent to a customer, not to a group chat. That person pays if the others disappear.
2. Everyone pays their share of month one, the admin fee, and the lock before move-in day. Note it as "storage, May to August."
3. Share the lock combination the day you load, not later.
4. Label every box with a name.
5. Decide now who closes the account in August. It is not automatically the last person still in town.
6. An even split is fair if you chose the room size together, even if one person has a fridge or a bike and the others have clothes. If one person needs the 10x10 and the others would have fit in a 5x5, split the extra rent, not the whole bill.

## When sharing stops being cheaper

Five boxes on Dorm Room Movers' prepaid rate are $409.50 plus $49.99 registration, for one person's boxes. Two people with five boxes each means two registrations and ten boxes. A shared room does not double when a second person adds boxes, until you outgrow it. Checked Nov 27, 2026.

Collegeboxes at the national $56 rate is $280 for five boxes, if that is the bill they send and if they do not add $37 a package. Two people at that rate are $560, which is more than the U-Haul 5x10 summer, and the pickup includes the hauling. Checked Nov 27, 2026. The U-Haul room price in that comparison was checked Nov 27, 2026. If neither of you has a car, that extra money is why you would still book the pickup. Run both totals before you assume the room wins.

Someone has to remember to move out. A 4-month pickup ends when they deliver. A unit bills until you close the account. Put the close-out date on a calendar the day you move in.
