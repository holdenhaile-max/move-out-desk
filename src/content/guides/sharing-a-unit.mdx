A pickup company charges each box to the person who owns it. A storage unit charges the room to whoever signed the lease. If you share the room, write the split down before anyone loads the car.

## Use a rate the page will stand behind

For a shared 5x10 that is labeled climate controlled, the defendable monthly numbers on November 18, 2026 were:

- U-Haul Texas Ave, $99.95, locked for 12 months, $0 admin fee.
- CubeSmart Manuel Drive, $100 in store. The $60 online price is only yours if they confirm the later months.
- Dowling Road, $74, with 50% off the first month and no published lock.
- SpareBox Earl Rudder, $32 promotional. Do not split a summer on that number until they confirm month two.

Four months at U-Haul is $399.80. Two people is $199.90 each. Three people is $133.27 each. That is before tax, which these pages do not show. The same four months at CubeSmart's in-store rate is $400, plus an admin fee that is not yet verified. Put the unknown fee in the shared pot when you learn it, instead of letting the person who signed the lease eat it.

The [calculator](/guides/texas-am-summer-storage) does this division. Set roommates to 2 or 3 and pick the unit rate. Leave "one month free" unchecked unless the facility confirmed it.

## A split that does not start a fight

1. One person signs, because the facilities we checked rent to a customer, not to a group chat. That person is on the hook if the others vanish.
2. Everyone pays their share of month one, the admin fee, and the lock before move-in day. Use a note that says "storage, May to August."
3. The lock combination is shared the day the unit is loaded, not later.
4. Boxes are labeled with a name. A shared unit is not a reason to skip labels. Pickup companies inventory items. A room does not.
5. The last person in town in August is not automatically the person who returns the lock and closes the account. Pick that person now.
6. If someone stores a mini fridge or a bike and everyone else stores clothes, the even split is still fair if you chose the room size together. If one person needs the 10x10 and the others would have fit in a 5x5, split the difference in the larger room's extra rent, not the whole bill.

## When sharing stops being cheaper

Five boxes on Dorm Room Movers' prepaid rate are $409.50 plus $49.99 registration, for one person's boxes. Two people with five boxes each is two registrations and ten boxes. A shared room does not double when a second person adds boxes, until you outgrow it.

Collegeboxes at the national $56 rate is $280 for five boxes, if that is the bill they send and if they do not add $37 a package. Two people at that rate are $560, which is more than the U-Haul 5x10 summer, and the pickup includes the hauling. If neither of you has a car, the extra money is the point of the pickup. Run both totals in the calculator before you assume the room wins.

Month-to-month also means someone must remember to vacate. A 4-month pickup contract ends when they deliver. A unit bills until the account is closed. Put the close-out date on a calendar the day you move in.
