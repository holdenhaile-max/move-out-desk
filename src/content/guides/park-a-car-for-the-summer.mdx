A campus permit and a storage lot are different products. If you are in town and need a place to park, start with Transportation Services. If the car has to sit while you are gone, use a lot that prints a parking price.

## If you are staying in town

Transportation Services says summer parking runs May 26, 2026 through August 9, 2026. Night permits are not valid during the day in that window. If you do not already have a permit, buy a Lot 100 permit at My Parking and park where the Break and Summer Parking Map allows. The price is prorated by the week. The page does not print a dollar amount. Checked Nov 26, 2026.

That page does not list a storage lot, and it does not say you can leave a car there for months while you are away.

## If the car has to sit

These are parking spaces, not rooms for boxes. A 10x20 room on another page is a storage room. Sort the table by price. Covered means a roof. Open means no roof.

**Dowling Road Storage, 10257 N. Dowling Road.** Open parking, 12 by 30 feet, is $89 a month. Covered parking, 12.5 by 42.5 feet, is $259 a month, and 2 were left. The page banner says 50% off the first month. The cards list the monthly rate before that discount, so ask which space gets it. Office hours are Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Gate access is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Phone is (979) 220-5578. Checked Nov 27, 2026.

**Lock N Roll Storage, 1501 Earl Rudder Freeway.** Open parking: a 10x20 is $83 with 1 left, a 12x20 is $94 with 3 left, a 12x30 is $112 with 4 left, a 12x40 is $145 with 3 left, and a 12x35 at $139 was not available to reserve. Covered parking: a 10x25 is $130 with 6 left, a 12x20 is $129 with 4 left, a 12x25 is $162 with 2 left, and a 12x40 at $220 was not available to reserve. The unit list charges a $24 administrative fee at move-in. The company pricing page says there is no admin fee beyond the first month and a small lock fee, and it does not print the lock amount. Phone is (979) 693-7655. Hours are still not listed, so call before you drive over. Checked Nov 26, 2026.

**SpareBox, 3201 Earl Rudder Freeway S.** Parking is $45 for a 10x20 and $59 for a 10x30. Both prices have an asterisk. The footnote says the rate is for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Neither space was available to reserve tonight. Phone is (979) 274-0001. Checked Nov 26, 2026.
