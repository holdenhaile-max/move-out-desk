A dorm's boxes, a mini fridge, and winter clothes usually fit a 5x5 or a 5x10. Sort the table by price. The lowest number on the screen is not always the bill in month two.

SpareBox and Dowling were checked Nov 24, 2026 on their own pages. Triangle was checked Nov 25, 2026 on its own page. CubeSmart on Manuel Drive was checked Nov 24, 2026 on Storage.com, because that page did not open. CubeSmart on Holleman Drive was checked Nov 25, 2026 on its own page for the rooms it listed. U-Haul was checked Nov 25, 2026. These are the lowest published prices for those two sizes. Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Drive-up means you can park at the door. Which building is closest to Northgate is the [Northgate page](/guides/storage-near-northgate).

**SpareBox, 3201 Earl Rudder Freeway S.** Drive-up 5x5 is $15. Climate 5x5 is $17. Drive-up 5x10 is $24. Climate 5x10 is $32. Each of those has an asterisk. The footnote says the rate is for new rentals, may not apply to every size, and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Checked Nov 24, 2026.

**CubeSmart, 219 Manuel Drive.** Drive-up 5x5 is $21.60 online and $36 in the store. Climate 5x5 is $24.60 online and $41 in the store. Drive-up 5x10 is $37.20 online and $62 in the store. Climate 5x10 is $57.60 online and $96 in the store. The banner says first month free and 40% off. The listing does not say the online price lasts four months. Use the in-store price unless they lock the online price in writing. Storage.com also lists a $29 admin fee and higher prices for other rooms of the same size. These are the lowest. Checked Nov 24, 2026.

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan.** The 5x5 is $42 and the page marked it sold out. The 5x10 is $46 and the page marked it in stock. Those lines do not say whether the room is heated and cooled. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

**Dowling Road Storage, 10257 N. Dowling Road.** Drive-up 5x10 is $49. Climate 5x5 and climate 5x10 are both $74. You get 50% off the first month. There is no price lock. If you were told Downing Road, this is the listing. Checked Nov 24, 2026.

**U-Haul, 2813 Texas Ave S.** These small rooms are interior, not drive-up. The non-climate 5x5 is $84.95. The non-climate 5x10 is $94.95. The climate 5x5 is $94.95. The climate 5x10 is $99.95. The monthly price is locked for 12 months and the admin fee is $0. An admin fee is a one-time charge at move-in. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. Checked Nov 25, 2026. SmartStop is on the same road at 3101 Texas Ave S. Those rooms are on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue).

- U-Haul climate 5x10 at $99.95 is $399.80, and the lock covers a summer. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

**Lock N Roll, 1501 Earl Rudder Freeway.** The unit list now shows prices. An interior 5x5 that is not climate controlled is $29. A climate 5x5 is $38. An interior 5x10 that is not climate controlled is $42. A climate 5x10 is $64. The unit card shows the monthly rate and does not print a separate move-in fee. The company page still says there is a small lock fee and does not print the amount. The $24 move-in line from Nov 21, 2026 was not on the cards. Checked Nov 24, 2026. Do not use the company starting price, such as a 5x5 at $39, as the College Station rate. What those sizes hold is on the [unit size guide](/guides/dorm-unit-sizes).

If the things you are storing cannot take a Texas summer, use a heated and cooled room even when a drive-up room is cheaper.
