A dorm's boxes, a mini fridge, and winter clothes usually fit a 5x5 or a 5x10. Sort the table by price. The lowest number on the screen is not always the bill in month two.

SpareBox, CubeSmart, Triangle, and Dowling were checked Nov 20, 2026. U-Haul was checked Nov 19, 2026. These are the lowest published prices for those two sizes. Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Drive-up means you can park at the door.

**SpareBox, 3201 Earl Rudder Freeway S.** Drive-up 5x5 is $15. Climate 5x5 is $17. Drive-up 5x10 is $24. Climate 5x10 is $32. Each of those has an asterisk. The footnote says the rate is for new rentals, may not apply to every size, and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**CubeSmart, 219 Manuel Drive.** Drive-up 5x5 is $21.60 online and $36 in the store. Climate 5x5 is $24.60 online and $41 in the store. Drive-up 5x10 is $42 online and $70 in the store. Climate 5x10 is $60 online and $100 in the store. The banner says first month free and 40% off. The page does not say the online price lasts four months. Use the in-store price unless they lock the online price in writing. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan.** The 5x5 is $42 and the page marked it sold out. The 5x10 is $46 and the page marked it in stock. Those lines do not say whether the room is heated and cooled. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**Dowling Road Storage, 10257 N. Dowling Road.** Drive-up 5x10 is $49. Climate 5x5 and climate 5x10 are both $74. You get 50% off the first month. There is no price lock. If you were told Downing Road, this is the listing. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

**U-Haul, 2813 Texas Ave S.** These small rooms are interior, not drive-up. The non-climate 5x5 is $84.95. The non-climate 5x10 is $94.95. The climate 5x5 is $94.95. The climate 5x10 is $99.95. The monthly price is locked for 12 months and the admin fee is $0. An admin fee is a one-time charge at move-in. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. Checked Nov 19, 2026.

Four months you can defend, before tax:

- SpareBox climate 5x10 at $32 is $128 only if that promo lasts. Do not budget $128 until they confirm month two. Checked Nov 20, 2026.
- CubeSmart climate 5x10 at the $100 in-store price is $400, plus an admin fee that is not printed. Checked Nov 20, 2026.
- Dowling climate 5x10 at $74 is $296. If the first month is half off, four months is $259. Later months are not locked. Checked Nov 20, 2026.
- U-Haul climate 5x10 at $99.95 is $399.80, and the lock covers a summer. Checked Nov 19, 2026.

CubeSmart on Holleman Drive did not list a 5x5 or a 5x10. Checked Nov 20, 2026, the list was a 10x20 drive-up at $99.60 online and $166 in the store, and a 20x20 drive-up at $205.80 online and $343 in the store. They say the store is less than three miles from campus. That does not help if the small room is not on the list.

Lock N Roll at 1501 Earl Rudder Freeway says it has 5x5 rooms and tells you to read the unit list. Checked Nov 20, 2026, that list still did not show a dollar amount. The price stays unverified. Call (979) 693-7655. Do not use a company-wide starting price as a College Station rate.

If the things you are storing cannot take a Texas summer, use a heated and cooled room even when a drive-up room is cheaper. That comparison is the [climate guide](/guides/college-station-climate-controlled). If nobody can drive, compare these rooms with a pickup in the [summer storage guide](/guides/texas-am-summer-storage).
