SmartStop is at 3101 Texas Avenue South. U-Haul's storage building is on the same road at 2813 Texas Avenue South. If you are pricing a room on Texas Ave, look at both before you pay.

**SmartStop, 3101 Texas Ave S.** A heated 5x5 is $18 online and $35 in the store, and 2 were left. A heated 5x10 is $29 online and $58 in the store, and that room is marked first month free. A 5x10 that is not heated is $20 online and $39 in the store, also first month free, with 3 left. The page lists that same pair again as a 10x5. You also pay a $29 admin fee, which is a one-time charge at move-in. The page does not say the online price lasts past the promotion. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Phone is (979) 335-8688.

**U-Haul, 2813 Texas Ave S.** The small rooms are inside the building, not at a drive-up door. A heated 5x5 is $94.95. A heated 5x10 is $99.95. The monthly price is locked for 12 months, which means it stays the same, and the admin fee is $0. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

Sort the table by price. Online is the lower number. In the store is the higher number. Use the in-store price for a summer unless they write the online price down for every month you will stay.

## What you pay on day one

SmartStop's move-in panel adds $29 on every room. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

- Heated 5x5: the first month on the panel is $18, plus $29, which is $47. Two rooms were left. Checked Nov 25, 2026.
- Heated 5x10: marked first month rent free, so the panel shows $0 rent plus the $29 fee. It does not say whether month two is $29 or $58. Checked Nov 25, 2026.
- Unheated 5x10: also first month free. The panel shows $0 plus $29. It does not name month two. The online price is $20 and the in-store price is $39. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

U-Haul does not add an admin fee. A heated 5x10 there is $99.95 in month one and in month two, because the lock is 12 months. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

A heated 10x10 at SmartStop is $45 online and $112 in the store. The page calls that room a limited time offer, and the list marks it 60% under the in-store price. A drive-up 10x10 is not heated. It is $52 online and $103 in the store, with 2 left. Drive-up means you can park at the door. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

A banner on the page says Black Friday savings up to 60% off. The heated 10x10 is the room at that 60%. The other small rooms are not. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

SmartStop's size guide says a 10x20 can hold a small car. A drive-up 10x20 is $74 online and $147 in the store, and it is marked first month free. That is their description, not a measurement of your car. Longer drive-up rooms are on the [College Station price list](/cities/college-station). Checked Nov 25, 2026.

Office hours are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and Sunday 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. The week shown on Nov 25, 2026 lists Thursday closed for a holiday. The gate is open 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. every day. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

The page lists a military discount and does not print the percent. That amount stays unverified.
