White Creek Apartments are at 225 Discovery Drive, on west campus. Residence Life lists that address, and the office phone is (979) 458-8500. Checked Nov 25, 2026. If you live there, the useful question is how far you have to drive, and what a small room costs when you get there.

No storage building is on Discovery Drive. Miles below are a driving route from the OpenStreetMap pin for 225 Discovery Drive to each building pin. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Your own drive can differ by a turn or two. Dowling Road is not in the mile list. A building pin for 10257 N. Dowling Road was not confirmed, so this page does not invent one.

Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Drive-up means you can park at the door.

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan. 2.32 miles.** This is the closest room with a published price. A 5x5 is $42 and the page marked it sold out. A 5x10 is $46 and the page marked it in stock. Those lines do not say whether the room is heated. The facility page does offer temperature-controlled indoor units and month-to-month storage for Texas A&M students. Ask which price is the heated room before you put clothes in it. Prices checked Nov 25, 2026. Phone is (979) 846-3000.

**CubeSmart, 104 Holleman Drive. 2.55 miles.** The own page listed drive-up rooms. A drive-up 10x10 is $54.60 online and $91 in the store, under first month free and 40% off. The page does not say the online price lasts past the first month. It did not list a 5x5 or a 5x10. It says the store is less than three miles from the Texas A&M campus. That is a campus claim, not a White Creek claim. Checked Nov 25, 2026. A 5x5 and a 5x10 from Storage.com are still in the table, with Nov 24, 2026 on those rows.

**U-Haul, 2813 Texas Ave S. 5.60 miles.** The small rooms are inside the building. A heated 5x5 is $94.95. A heated 5x10 is $99.95. The monthly price is locked for 12 months, and the admin fee is $0. An admin fee is a one-time charge at move-in. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

**SmartStop, 3101 Texas Ave S. 5.89 miles.** A heated 5x5 is $18 online and $35 in the store, and 2 were left. A heated 5x10 is $29 online and $58 in the store, and that room is marked first month free. You also pay a $29 admin fee. The page does not say the online price lasts past the promotion. A drive-up 10x10 is not heated. It is $52 online and $103 in the store, with 2 left. Checked Nov 25, 2026. The rest of that building is on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue).

CubeSmart on Manuel Drive is 3.88 miles. Lock N Roll is 4.48 miles. SpareBox on Earl Rudder is 6.67 miles. This page does not repeat prices that were not opened today. The table shows each of those prices with the day it was checked.

The Storage Spot, 1485 Old Arrington Road, says it has climate-controlled units, drive-up units, and 24-hour access, and it names Texas A&M. The page does not print a monthly price. Checked Nov 25, 2026. Phone is (979) 977-1324. This page does not add a mile, because a building pin for that street number was not confirmed.
