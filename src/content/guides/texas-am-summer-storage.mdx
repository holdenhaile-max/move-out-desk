If you can drive, and a roommate will share the lock, a unit is usually the cheaper summer. If nobody has a car, or the boxes have to come back to the room on a set day, use a pickup. If you are not coming back, ship it home.

Call before you treat a pickup as booked. Checked Nov 22, 2026, Collegeboxes listed Texas A&M pickups for December 11, 2026 and May 10, 2027. The same page says full-service moving (they pick up from the room) is currently unavailable. Hall closing is a different date, and Residence Life has not posted the Fall 2026 clock time. That page is the [move-out checklist](/guides/texas-am-dorm-move-out). The fee-by-fee version is the [Collegeboxes guide](/guides/collegeboxes-texas-am).

## Pickup: they haul, you pay per box

Collegeboxes and Dorm Room Movers both have a Texas A&M page. Neither is a university office. Collegeboxes says so: no active partnership with Texas A&M University, College Station.

You pay per item for a set term so you do not rent a truck, buy a lock, or come back to campus.

A standard Collegeboxes box is $56 for 4 months on the national list, and that list includes pickup and delivery. A Dorm Room Movers box on the Texas A&M list is $91 for the same term, or $81.90 if you pay up front and take the published 10% off. Dorm Room Movers also charges $49.99 to register, which includes five of their boxes and tape. Collegeboxes sells a supply kit for $39.99 plus tax and says its storage list has no registration fee. Collegeboxes checked Nov 22, 2026. Dorm Room Movers checked Nov 22, 2026.

The box price is not the whole bill. The Collegeboxes school page adds a $37 handling fee per package it receives. A handling fee is the charge for receiving a box. The national page lists $30 for Ship to School. Dorm Room Movers' pricing feed lists $19.99 to handle a box you ship home. The public page formats that amount with no cents, so it shows as $20. The two do not match, and UPS freight is still not listed. Checked Nov 22, 2026.

## A unit: you haul, you pay for the room

You pack, drive, and lock it. You can open it during access hours. The price does not change with the number of boxes until you need a bigger room.

For books and clothes, look at a 5x5 and a 5x10. CubeSmart calls a 5x5 a hall closet and a 5x10 a walk-in closet. U-Haul at Texas Ave, the building Collegeboxes names, lists a climate-controlled 5x10 (heated and cooled) at $99.95 a month. The price is locked for 12 months and the admin fee is $0. An admin fee is a one-time charge when you move in. CubeSmart on Manuel Drive lists a climate-controlled 5x10 at $57.60 online and $96 in the store, under a banner that says first month free and 40% off. The page does not say the $57.60 lasts four months. CubeSmart checked Nov 22, 2026. U-Haul checked Nov 23, 2026. SmartStop is the other storage building on Texas Ave, at 3101. Those prices are on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue).

The lowest 5x5 and 5x10 prices are on the [cheapest storage page](/guides/cheapest-storage-near-texas-am). What a 5x5 and a 5x10 hold, in the companies' own words, is the [unit size guide](/guides/dorm-unit-sizes). The full climate list, including SpareBox, Dowling Road, and rooms that are not labeled, is the [climate comparison](/guides/college-station-climate-controlled).

## Shipping home: one way

Ship it if you are done with the things until you are home, or if you are not coming back to the same room.

Collegeboxes publishes estimates, not a quote to your address. These Ship Home ranges say there is no handling fee. Checked Nov 22, 2026:

- In-state, $45 to $60 per box, and that section says there is no handling fee.
- Regional, $60 to $90 per box. The page uses that word and does not list the states.
- Coast to coast, $75 to $100 per box.

Your price still depends on destination, size, and weight. A box of books at 50 pounds or more can add $25 to $50 on the Collegeboxes fee list. Dorm Room Movers will pick up boxes and hand them to UPS, but the freight amount is not on the fee list. The pricing feed lists $19.99 for that handling fee, and the public page shows it as $20. Checked Nov 22, 2026. Neither company has published a quote to a specific hometown. Ship to School is a different Collegeboxes list, and those ranges already include the $30 handling fee: $75 to $90 in-state, $90 to $120 regional, and $105 to $130 coast to coast. Checked Nov 22, 2026.

## What four months costs

Five standard boxes is a small dorm, not a whole apartment. Change the numbers in the calculator under the example.

**Collegeboxes, national box rate only.** 5 times $56 is $280 for the 4-month term. That assumes they bill the national storage rate and do not add the $37 handling fee. The school page does not confirm either one.

**Collegeboxes, if the $37 fee is on every box.** 5 times $56 plus 5 times $37 is $465. The pages do not say the fees stack. Ask.

**Dorm Room Movers, paid up front.** 5 times $81.90 is $409.50, plus the $49.99 registration fee, which is $459.49. At the list price, five boxes are $455 plus $49.99, which is $504.99. Their own example matches the box math: $455 list, $409.50 prepaid, before registration.

**A climate 5x10 you rent yourself.** U-Haul Texas Ave is $99.95 times 4, which is $399.80, with the price lock and a $0 admin fee. CubeSmart's in-store rate is $96 times 4, which is $384, before an admin fee the page does not print. Split by two roommates, that room is $192 a person, and it holds more than five boxes. CubeSmart's $57.60 online rate would be $230.40 for four months only if the discount lasts. The page does not say that it does. CubeSmart checked Nov 22, 2026. U-Haul checked Nov 23, 2026.

**Shipping five boxes in-state.** $225 to $300, and the boxes do not come back. That range is an estimate, not your quote.

## Which one to book

Use a pickup if nobody can drive a loaded car and you need the boxes back in the room. Call Collegeboxes and ask which service is actually open. Ask Dorm Room Movers for the featured dates. They are behind a sign-in. An off-date with Dorm Room Movers is $30 to $499. An off-date with Collegeboxes is $500 on the national fee list.

Use a shared unit if two or more of you can haul once in May and once in August, especially if you have a mini fridge, a bicycle, or a futon. Those are extra lines on a pickup bill ($82 and $88 at Collegeboxes, $113 and $130 at Dorm Room Movers). Inside a unit they are just things in a room you already rented. Prefer a price the place will lock. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same for the months they name. U-Haul Texas Ave is the one here that prints a 12-month lock. Use the in-store price when a discount does not say how long it lasts.

Ship home if you are not coming back, or if a quote for a few boxes lands inside or under the Collegeboxes range. Get that quote. Do not budget the range as if it were a bill.

A few weeks, such as a TV and a dresser between July 31 and August 15, is a bad fit for a 4-month pickup. Rent month to month. The first month may be discounted. The admin fee may wipe that out. Several places do not list that fee. The city table marks it.

## Ask before you pay

- Will Collegeboxes pick up at Texas A&M while the page says full-service moving is unavailable?
- Are $56 and $37 added together?
- What are Dorm Room Movers' next free pickup dates, and where is the warehouse?
- What is UPS or FedEx freight to your home address?
- What is sales tax on the total?
- How long does CubeSmart's online discount last?
- How far is the drive? Collegeboxes says the Texas Ave U-Haul is just over 3 miles from campus. CubeSmart says Holleman Drive is less than three miles. The others do not say.

If a parent is paying, send the source link on the row. A company can change a rate after the day shown on its row without telling this site.
