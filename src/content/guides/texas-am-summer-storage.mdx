You have three ways to leave a room in College Station. A company can pick the boxes up and bring them back. You can rent a storage unit and haul the load yourself. Or you can ship the boxes home and be done with them until you are there.

The right one depends on whether you have a car, whether roommates will share a lock, and whether the pickup company will actually come. On November 18, 2026, Collegeboxes listed Texas A&M pickups for December 11, 2026 and May 10, 2027. The same page says full-service moving is currently unavailable. Read both sentences before you treat a pickup as booked. The fee-by-fee version of that problem is the [Collegeboxes guide](/guides/collegeboxes-texas-am).

## What a pickup service is selling

Collegeboxes and Dorm Room Movers both publish a Texas A&M College Station page. Neither is a university office. Collegeboxes says so on its own page: no active partnership with Texas A&M University, College Station.

A pickup price is per item, for a term, and it is supposed to include the truck. You are paying so you do not rent a vehicle, buy a lock, or come back to campus to fetch the boxes.

On the national Collegeboxes price list, a standard box is $56 for a 4-month term, and that list says pickup and delivery are included. A DRM box on the Texas A&M price feed is $91 for the same length of term, or $81.90 if you prepay the term and take the published 10% off. DRM also charges a $49.99 registration fee, which includes five of their boxes and tape. Collegeboxes sells a supply kit for $39.99 plus tax and says its storage list has no registration fee.

Those box rates are not the whole bill. Collegeboxes' school page adds a $37 handling fee per package it receives. Its national page lists a $30 handling fee for Ship to School. Dorm Room Movers' ship-home handling fee is $19.99 per box, and the UPS freight on top of that is not published.

## What a local unit is selling

A unit is a room you rent by the month. You pack, you drive, you lock it, and you can open it during access hours. The price does not care how many boxes you fit inside, until you need a larger room.

For a summer of books and clothes, the useful comparison sizes are a 5x5 and a 5x10. CubeSmart's own size guide describes a 5x5 as a hall closet and a 5x10 as a walk-in closet. U-Haul at Texas Ave, the building Collegeboxes names, lists a climate-controlled 5x10 at $99.95 a month with a 12-month price lock and a $0 admin fee. CubeSmart on Manuel Drive lists a climate-controlled 5x10 at $60 online and $100 in store, under a banner that says first month free and 40% off. The page does not say the $60 lasts four months.

The full climate table, including SpareBox, Dowling Road, and the facilities whose rates are not labeled, is the [climate comparison](/guides/college-station-climate-controlled).

## What shipping home is selling

Shipping is a one-way trip. It fits if you are finished with the things until you are home, or if you are not returning to the same room.

Collegeboxes publishes estimates, not a quote to your address:

- In-state, $45 to $60 per box, and that section says there is no handling fee.
- Regional, $60 to $90 per box.
- Coast to coast, $75 to $100 per box.

The price still depends on destination, size, and weight. A box of books at 50 pounds or more can add $25 to $50 on the Collegeboxes fee schedule. Dorm Room Movers will pick up boxes and hand them to UPS, but the freight amount is not on the public fee list. Only the $19.99 handling fee is. A hometown quote is not yet verified for either company.

## A four-month example you can change

Five standard boxes is a small dorm, not a whole apartment. Here is the arithmetic from the published point prices. The calculator under the example uses the same inputs.

**Collegeboxes, national box rate only.** 5 times $56 is $280 for the 4-month term. That assumes the school will bill the national storage rate and will not add the $37 handling fee. The school page does not confirm either assumption.

**Collegeboxes, if the $37 handling fee is charged on every box.** 5 times $56 plus 5 times $37 is $465. Treat this as a what-if. The pages do not say the fees stack.

**Dorm Room Movers, prepaid.** 5 times $81.90 is $409.50, plus the $49.99 registration fee, which is $459.49. At the list price, five boxes are $455 plus $49.99, which is $504.99. Their own example of five DRM boxes matches the box math: $455 list, $409.50 prepaid, before registration.

**A climate 5x10 you rent yourself.** U-Haul Texas Ave is $99.95 times 4, which is $399.80, with the price lock and a $0 admin fee published. CubeSmart's in-store rate is $100 times 4, which is $400, before an admin fee the page does not print. Split by two roommates, that room is about $200 a person, and it holds more than five boxes. CubeSmart's $60 online rate would be $240 for four months only if the discount survives. That duration is not yet verified.

**Shipping five boxes in-state, using the published range.** $225 to $300, and the boxes do not come back. That range is an estimate, not your quote.

## How to choose with only these facts

Use a pickup service if nobody in the room can drive a loaded car, and if you need the boxes delivered back to a room. Call Collegeboxes and ask which service is actually open, because the page contradicts itself. Ask Dorm Room Movers for the featured dates, because they are behind a sign-in. An off-date with Dorm Room Movers is published as $30 to $499. An off-date with Collegeboxes is $500 on the national fee list.

Use a shared unit if two or more people can haul once in May and once in August, and if the things include a mini fridge, a bicycle, or a futon. Those items are extra line items on a pickup bill ($82 and $88 at Collegeboxes, $113 and $130 at Dorm Room Movers) and they are just objects inside a room you already rented. Prefer a rate the facility will lock. U-Haul Texas Ave is the one in this set that prints a 12-month lock. Prefer the in-store price when a promo does not say how long it lasts.

Ship home if you are not returning, or if the load is a few boxes and the destination quote lands inside or under the Collegeboxes range. Get that quote. Do not budget the range as if it were an invoice.

A gap of a few weeks, like the old question about storing a TV and a dresser between July 31 and August 15, is a bad fit for a 4-month pickup contract. A month-to-month unit is the product those companies are describing. The first month may be discounted. The admin fee may erase the discount. That fee is unpublished at several facilities, which is why the city table marks it.
