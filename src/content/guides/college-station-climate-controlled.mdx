Students asking about summer storage in College Station usually have the same load in mind: books, winter clothes, a mini fridge, sometimes a bike. Those are the things a hot room can ruin, and they are the reason a climate-controlled unit shows up in the question. This page only calls a unit climate controlled when the rate line says so.

One building publishes a temperature. U-Haul Moving & Storage at Texas Ave says it keeps temperatures between 65 and 85 degrees year-round, depending on the system, and that the building is humidity controlled. Everyone else in this set says "climate," "climate controlled," or "temperature control" without a number. That missing number is marked, not invented.

## Rooms whose rates are labeled climate controlled

These rows are the ones a summer comparison can use. The in-store or single monthly price is the figure to budget unless the facility also locks the cheaper price in writing.

Reading the small sizes, which are the ones that fit a dorm:

**U-Haul Texas Ave, 2813 Texas Ave S.** Climate 5x5 is $94.95. Climate 5x10 is $99.95. Climate 10x10 is $179.95. The monthly price is locked for 12 months. The admin fee is $0. There is no deposit. A free lock comes with online move-in and is valued at $11.95. Access is 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. This is also the building Collegeboxes says it uses for Texas A&M storage. Renting a room here yourself is a different product from paying Collegeboxes per box.

**CubeSmart, 219 Manuel Drive.** Climate 5x5 is $24.60 online and $41 in store. Climate 5x10 is $60 online and $100 in store. Climate 10x10 is $91.20 online and $152 in store. Each of those lines is under "First month free and 40% off." The in-store 5x10 is a dollar away from U-Haul's locked price. The online price is the one that looks cheap, and the page never says it is the price in month two. Office hours are Monday to Friday 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Saturday 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Access is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Insurance is required. The admin fee amount is not published. A 5% discount for active-duty military at move-in is published. Driving distance is not.

**SpareBox, 3201 Earl Rudder Freeway S.** Climate 5x5 is $17, climate 5x10 is $32, and climate 10x10 is $56. All of those are asterisk prices. The footnote says they are promotional, for new rentals, may not apply to every size, and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. An administrative fee applies and the amount is not printed. This is the address older directories give for All Seasons. All Seasons' own directions page returned a 404 the day we checked, so SpareBox is the operator with a rate card. Access is 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily.

**Dowling Road Storage, 10257 N. Dowling Road.** Climate 5x5 and climate 5x10 are both $74. Climate 10x10 is $119. The listing offers 50% off the first month. It does not offer a price lock. People spell this road Downing. The listing spells it Dowling. Office hours are Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Access is 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. The admin fee is not on the listing.

A four-month budget at the rate you can defend looks like this, before tax:

- U-Haul climate 5x10: $99.95 times 4 is $399.80, and the lock covers a summer.
- CubeSmart climate 5x10 at the in-store rate: $400, plus an unknown admin fee, plus required insurance of unknown price.
- Dowling climate 5x10: $74 times 4 is $296, and the first month is half off if the promo applies, which would make the four months $259. Later months are not described as locked.
- SpareBox climate 5x10: $32 times 4 is $128 only if a promotional rate survives. The footnote says it might not. Budgeting $128 for the summer is not yet supported.

## Facilities that talk about climate control and do not give a labeled rate

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan.** The page is explicit that the facility offers temperature-controlled indoor units, and it tells Texas A&M students they can leave items between semesters on a month-to-month lease. The prices in the page data are real: a 5x10 at $46, a 10x10 at $70, both marked in stock when we read them. The rate lines do not say, in plain language, which of those prices is the temperature-controlled room. Those rates are left out of the climate table on purpose. Access is 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day. Phone is (979) 846-3000.

## What to ask before you pick the cheap climate price

1. Is this unit on the climate system, and what temperature do you hold in summer?
2. Is the price on the screen the price in months two, three, and four?
3. What is the admin fee, the lock, and the required insurance, in dollars?
4. What are the access hours in May, when everyone moves out at once?
5. Do you take a delivery if a parent ships a box to the unit? Holleman and Dowling and Lock N Roll say deliveries are accepted. Confirm the others.

U-Haul Texas Ave is the only building in this set that already answers the temperature question and the price-lock question on the public page. SpareBox is the lowest labeled climate price and the one whose footnote most clearly warns you not to assume the price sticks. CubeSmart Manuel Drive is the direct comparison to U-Haul on a 5x10: about $100 if you ignore the promo, $60 if the promo is real for the whole summer.
