If you are storing books, winter clothes, a mini fridge, or a bike, get a room that is heated and cooled. Heat can wreck those. Use the in-store or single monthly price unless the place locks the cheaper price in writing. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same for the months they name.

Only U-Haul at Texas Ave states a temperature: 65 to 85 degrees year-round, depending on the system, and the building controls humidity. Everyone else says "climate" or "temperature control" without a number. Ask what they hold in July.

## Ask this before you take the cheap price

1. Is this unit heated and cooled, and what temperature do you hold in summer?
2. Is the price on the screen the price in months two, three, and four?
3. What is the admin fee, the lock, and the required insurance, in dollars?
4. What are the access hours in May, when everyone moves at once?
5. Will you take a box a parent ships to the unit? Holleman, Dowling, and Lock N Roll say yes. Confirm the others.

U-Haul Texas Ave is the only building here that already answers the temperature and the price lock. SpareBox is the lowest labeled climate price, and the footnote tells you not to assume it sticks. CubeSmart on Manuel Drive is the direct comparison to U-Haul on a 5x10: about $100 if you ignore the promo, $60 if the promo lasts all summer.
