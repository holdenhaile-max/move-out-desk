A few weeks is a different bill from a summer. If you can drive, rent a room by the month and leave when the next lease starts. A pickup company bills you for 4 months, even if you only need the boxes for the gap between apartments.

Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Drive-up means you can park at the door. Each price below names the day it was checked. Sort the table if you want every 5x5 and 5x10. Each row shows its own day.

**Lock N Roll, 1501 Earl Rudder Freeway.** A climate 5x10 is $64. A climate 5x5 is $38. The page says the lease is month to month. The unit list also charges a $24 administrative fee at move-in. The company pricing page says there is no admin fee beyond the first month and a small lock fee, and it does not print the lock amount. Checked Nov 26, 2026.

**Dowling Road Storage, 10257 N. Dowling Road.** A climate 5x5 and a climate 5x10 are both $74. The listing takes 50% off the first month, so month one is $37 of rent before tax. The reserve panel says you pay month to month. It does not print an admin fee. An admin fee is a one-time charge when you move in. If you were told Downing Road, this is the listing. Checked Nov 26, 2026.

**SpareBox, 3201 Earl Rudder Freeway S.** A climate 5x10 is $31. A climate 5x5 is $17. Both have an asterisk. The footnote says the rate is for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. The page does not say a minimum number of months. It says an admin fee applies and does not print the amount. For a stay of one month, ask what hits the card on day one, and what month two would be if your dates slip. Checked Nov 26, 2026.

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan.** A 5x10 is $46 and the page marked it in stock. A 5x5 is $42 and the page marked it sold out. Those lines do not say whether the room is heated. The page does say it holds items for Texas A&M students between semesters, on month-to-month leases. Ask which price is the heated room before you put clothes in it. Checked Nov 25, 2026.

**CubeSmart, 219 Manuel Drive.** A climate 5x10 is $57.60 online and $96 in the store. A climate 5x5 is $24.60 online and $41 in the store. The banner says first month free and 40% off. It does not say the online price lasts past the promotion. Storage.com lists a $29 admin fee, due when you move in, and says insurance is required. It does not print the insurance price. CubeSmart's own page lists the same lowest prices, names an administrative fee and a protection plan, and does not print those dollar amounts. These prices are the lowest rooms of that size. Other climate 5x10 rooms on Storage.com were as high as $67.20 online. Checked Nov 26, 2026.

**CubeSmart, 104 Holleman Drive.** Checked Nov 24, 2026, on Storage.com, no heated room was on the list. The lowest drive-up 5x10 is $31.80 online and $53 in the store. The lowest drive-up 5x5 is $15.60 online and $26 in the store. Storage.com lists a $29 admin fee. Other drive-up 5x5 rooms were listed up to $18 online. Checked Nov 25, 2026, CubeSmart's own page did not list a 5x5 or a 5x10. It did list a drive-up 10x10 at $54.60 online and $91 in the store.

## A pickup is a 4-month bill

Collegeboxes prices a standard box at $56 for 4 months on the national list. The Texas A&M page adds a $37 handling fee per package it receives, and the national Ship to School list says $30. The pages do not say those fees stack with the $56. The same school page says full-service room pickup is currently unavailable, and it still lists pickup on December 11, 2026 and May 10, 2027. Checked Nov 24, 2026.

Dorm Room Movers prices a box at $91 for 4 months, or $81.90 if you pay the term up front. Registration is $49.99. Checked Nov 24, 2026. Five boxes at the prepaid rate are $409.50 plus registration. That is the wrong product for a TV and a dresser between July 31 and August 15, or for a coat you need while a residence hall is locked.

If you are not coming back to College Station at all, shipping home is the other choice. Those ranges are on the [summer storage page](/guides/texas-am-summer-storage). Hall closing dates, and what you can leave in a dorm room, are on the [move-out page](/guides/texas-am-dorm-move-out). Residence Life's posted dates there were checked Nov 20, 2026.

## What one month can actually be

Use the in-store price, or the single monthly price, unless the place locks a cheaper number in writing.

- Lock N Roll climate 5x10: $64, plus a $24 administrative fee on the unit list. The company pricing page disagrees and still does not print a lock amount. Checked Nov 26, 2026.
- Dowling climate 5x10: $37 of rent if the 50% first month applies to a one-month stay. Later months would be $74, and they are not locked. No admin fee is printed. Checked Nov 26, 2026.
- SpareBox climate 5x10: $31 only as a promo. Do not assume a second month stays at $31. Checked Nov 26, 2026.
- Triangle 5x10: $46, and the line does not say the room is heated. Checked Nov 24, 2026.
- CubeSmart Manuel climate 5x10: $96 in the store, or $57.60 online if they honor it. First month free does not mean day one is $0. Storage.com still lists the $29 admin fee, and insurance on top, with no insurance price. Checked Nov 26, 2026.

A price for four months, and which cheap rate may change in month two, is on the [cheapest storage page](/guides/cheapest-storage-near-texas-am).
