Callaway House is a private dorm-style apartment at 301 George Bush Drive West, College Station, TX 77840. The office phone is (979) 260-7700. The page says the office is open Monday through Sunday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and to call about holiday hours. It lists academic term leases, and it says the complex is on the Texas A&M bus route. It does not name the route, and it does not say whether you have to empty the room over winter break. Checked Nov 28, 2026.

If you live at Northgate, use the [Northgate page](/guides/storage-near-northgate). If you live at White Creek, use the [White Creek page](/guides/storage-near-white-creek). If you live at the Gardens, use the [Gardens page](/guides/storage-near-the-gardens). The miles from those places are different.

No storage building is at 301 George Bush Drive West. Miles below are a driving route from the OpenStreetMap pin for that address. SmartStop's mile uses the pin printed on SmartStop's own page. Checked Nov 28, 2026. Your own drive can differ by a turn or two. Dowling Road is not in the mile list. A building pin for 10257 N. Dowling Road was not confirmed, so this page does not invent one.

Climate controlled means the room is heated and cooled. Drive-up means you can park at the door.

**CubeSmart, 104 Holleman Drive. 0.83 miles.** This is the closest room with a published price. The rooms on the list are drive-up, not heated. A 5x5 is $15.60 online and $26 in the store. A 5x10 is $31.80 online and $53 in the store. The banner says first month free and 40% off. The listing does not say the online price lasts past that promotion. Use the in-store price unless they lock the online price in writing. Storage.com lists a $29 admin fee. An admin fee is a one-time charge at move-in. CubeSmart's own page did not open. Checked Nov 28, 2026.

**Triangle Storage, 3810 Old College Road, Bryan. 2.41 miles.** A 5x5 is $42 and the page marked it sold out. A 5x10 is $46 and the page marked it in stock. Those lines do not say whether the room is heated. The facility page does offer temperature-controlled indoor units and month-to-month storage for Texas A&M students. Ask which price is the heated room before you put clothes in it. Prices checked Nov 28, 2026. Phone is (979) 846-3000.

**CubeSmart, 219 Manuel Drive. 2.42 miles.** This is the closest room that labels a heated price. Climate 5x5 is $24.60 online and $41 in the store. Climate 5x10 is $57.60 online and $96 in the store. The banner says first month free and 40% off. Use $96 for a climate 5x10 unless they lock the online price in writing. Storage.com lists a $29 admin fee and says insurance is required. It does not print the insurance price. CubeSmart's own page did not open. Prices checked Nov 28, 2026.

**U-Haul, 2813 Texas Ave S. 3.68 miles.** The small rooms are inside the building. A heated 5x10 is $99.95 a month, locked for 12 months, and the admin fee is $0. A price lock means the monthly price stays the same. Checked Nov 28, 2026.

**Lock N Roll, 1501 Earl Rudder Freeway. 3.82 miles.** A climate 5x5 is $38. A climate 5x10 is $64. The unit list also charges a $24 administrative fee at move-in. The company pricing page says there is no admin fee beyond the first month and a small lock fee, and it does not print the lock amount. Prices checked Nov 28, 2026. Phone is (979) 693-7655. Hours are still not listed, so call before you plan to get in.

**SmartStop, 3101 Texas Ave S. 3.98 miles.** The public offer list did not include a heated 5x5. The other rooms, including a heated 5x10 at $29 online, are on the [Texas Avenue page](/guides/smartstop-texas-avenue). The offer list was checked Nov 28, 2026. The in-store price was not on that list.

**SpareBox, 3201 Earl Rudder Freeway S. 4.73 miles.** A climate 5x5 is $17. A climate 5x10 is $31. Both have an asterisk. The footnote says the rate is for new rentals and that rent can change with 30 days' notice. Checked Nov 28, 2026.

If the things cannot take the heat, use a heated room even when Holleman is closer. Holleman's list does not include a heated room. Manuel Drive does, and U-Haul prints a temperature band and a 12-month lock. That list is the [climate page](/guides/college-station-climate-controlled).
